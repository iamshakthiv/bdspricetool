# Size & Price Calculator

A practical tool for calculating product dimensions, converting measurement units, and estimating prices based on size and pricing rules.

## Overview

This project aims to simplify the process of measuring products, converting dimensions between different units, and calculating prices accurately. It is designed for anyone who works with custom-sized products, materials, printing, backdrops, displays, or other items where dimensions affect the final cost.

## Key Features

- **Measurement Conversion:** Convert between millimeters (mm), centimeters (cm), meters (m), inches (in), and feet (ft).
- **Size Calculator:** Enter product dimensions such as width and height to calculate total size and area.
- **Price Calculator:** Estimate product costs using dimensions, area, unit rates, and configurable pricing rules.
- **Multiple Measurement Units:** Work with metric and imperial measurements without manually converting values.
- **Custom Dimensions:** Calculate prices for products made to specific width and height requirements.
- **Accurate Calculations:** Use consistent conversion factors and calculation methods to reduce manual errors.
- **Flexible Pricing:** Support different pricing rates, minimum charges, and other configurable pricing options.
- **User-Friendly Interface:** Make measurements and pricing calculations simple, clear, and efficient.

## How It Will Be Used

1. Enter the required product width and height.
2. Select the measurement unit used for the dimensions.
3. Convert the measurements into another unit when needed.
4. Calculate the total area or size of the product.
5. Enter the applicable price per unit or area.
6. Calculate the estimated total price based on the selected dimensions and pricing rules.
7. Review the results and adjust the dimensions or pricing inputs as required.

## Purpose

The goal is to create a reliable, easy-to-use calculator that saves time, reduces calculation errors, and makes product sizing and price estimation more efficient.
